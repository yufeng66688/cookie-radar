# Cookie Radar — X Thread Draft

## Post 1 / hook

Cookie Radar is live: a Cookie Chain cApp that turns every public Memo into a live
activity feed — and lets you post one signed crumb yourself. 🍪

## Post 2 / problem

Cookie Chain is fast, Solana-compatible, and cheap, but it can feel hard to see what
is actually happening. Cookie Radar fixes that: live RPC health, slot, epoch, TPS,
validators, COOK price, and a live Memo radar in one page.

## Post 3 / product

What you can do:

✅ Connect Nightly
✅ Switch Nightly to Cookie Chain automatically
✅ See your address, COOK balance, and DAS assets
✅ Post an on-chain crumb with one signature
✅ Watch Sign → Send → Confirm live
✅ Explore the largest CookieSwap / Cookiebox pools
✅ Quote and sign COOK ↔ bCOOK routes through Cookiebox

## Post 4 / how to use

How to try it:

1. Install Nightly
2. Open Cookie Radar
3. Click Connect Nightly
4. Approve the network switch
5. Type a crumb and sign
6. Open the txn in Cookiescan

No seed phrase. No custody. No backend.

## Post 5 / bridge

First time on Cookie Chain? Bridge a small amount of COOK from Solana:
https://bridge.cookiescan.io

Then come back and send your first on-chain crumb.
